## Concept marker

Fiduciary Computation (FC) studies explicit computational boundaries between information and protected consequences.

The public working distinction is:

```text
Information
  ≠ Proposal
  ≠ Consequential Admission
  ≠ Consequential Authority
  ≠ Protected Execution
  ≠ Outcome
```

A system is studied as fiduciary with respect to a protected consequence when the dependencies permitted to affect that consequence are made explicit and bounded at a declared computational boundary.
